# excel-superstore-dashboard

Analyzing Vrinda Store sales data to understand sales trends, customer demographics, order status, top-performing states, and sales channels using Microsoft Excel.

# Overview

This project analyzes Vrinda Store's sales data through an interactive Excel dashboard. The dashboard provides a clear overview of monthly sales, order volume, customer gender and age distribution, order status, top-performing states, and sales channels.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can help understand customer behavior, identify strong-performing regions and channels, and support data-driven sales decisions.

# Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze monthly sales and order trends.
* Compare sales between men and women.
* Understand the distribution of delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
* Identify the top-performing states based on sales.
* Analyze customer distribution across different age groups and genders.
* Compare order volume across different sales channels.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for exploring sales performance using filters.

# Dataset

The project uses the Vrinda Store data analysis dataset containing customer, order, sales, channel, category, and geographic information.

# Data Cleaning & Preparation

Before creating the dashboard, the sales data was prepared and organized for analysis.

The preparation process included:

* Reviewing the sales dataset for consistency.
* Organizing sales, order, customer, category, channel, and geographic fields.
* Preparing data for Pivot Table analysis.
* Creating calculated summaries for monthly sales and order trends.
* Grouping customers by age and gender for demographic analysis.
* Aggregating sales by state to identify the top-performing regions.
* Preparing order status and channel-level summaries for visualization.

# Key Findings

* Gender Distribution: Women contribute a larger share of sales, accounting for approximately **64%**, compared with **36%** from men.
* Order Status: The majority of orders are successfully delivered, representing approximately **92%** of the displayed order status distribution.
* Top State: **Maharashtra** is the highest-selling state among the top five states shown in the dashboard.
* State Performance: Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu are also among the top-performing states.
* Sales Channels: **Amazon** contributes the largest share of orders at approximately **35%**, followed by Myntra and Flipkart.
* Monthly Performance: Sales and order volumes vary across months, with **March** showing one of the strongest performances in the displayed monthly analysis.
* Customer Demographics: Adults represent the largest customer group in the displayed age-based analysis.

# Visualizations

The interactive Excel dashboard contains the following visualizations:

* **Orders vs Sales** – Monthly comparison of sales amount and order count.
* **Sales: Men Vs Women** – Gender-based sales distribution.
* **Order Status** – Distribution of delivered, cancelled, returned, and refunded orders.
* **Sales: Top 5 States** – Sales comparison among the five highest-performing states.
* **Age Vs Gender** – Comparison of customer age groups by gender.
* **Orders: Channels** – Distribution of orders across different sales channels.

The dashboard also includes interactive slicers for:
